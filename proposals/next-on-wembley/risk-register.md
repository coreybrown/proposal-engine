# Risk register: Next on Wembley

This is a new-product run that stopped after Stage 2, an early exit on the competitive-strategist's KILL. It is a rerun that incorporates `clarifications.md`, the decision-maker's argument that the lack of automatic watch-history capture is the biggest risk. That argument is treated as the decision-maker's view, not as evidence (see the coverage note for what it changed and why).

There is no business case, economics audit or fact check. Nothing below has been fact-checked, and the digest's drift check did not run. The register is built from the 46 `RISKS:` entries reproduced in `research/_digest.md`, consolidated to 17 rows. Rows are sorted by severity, then confidence, and within a tier by how directly each drives the verdict. Lenses follow `docs/new-product-contract.md` §6, two at most.
